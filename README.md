### 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer graduated from the **State University of Pará (UEPA)** and currently pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Science at **UFPA, Brazil**.

I have professional experience developing **web and cross-platform applications**, working with Flutter, React, TypeScript, Node.js, RESTful APIs, databases, and cloud services.

In academic research, I work with **Python, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and experimental software development**.

My current research investigates the application of **Large Language Models (LLMs) and Web Agents to Exploratory Software Testing**, focusing on automated test scenario generation and execution.
